About Visa Success Connect

We exist to make visa guidance transparent, trustworthy, and accessible for every student dreaming of studying abroad.

Our mission

Students face uncertainty when choosing consultancies—unverified agents, hidden fees, and poor outcomes. Visa Success Connect verifies consultancies, provides free tools like the visa checker, and connects students with trusted partners.

What we do

  • Verify education consultancies for transparency and quality
  • Offer a free visa chance checker to help students self-assess
  • Publish visa news and guides to keep students informed
  • Provide scholarship support through our partner network

Recognition

Awarded in the Big Idea Challenge 2024 by London Metropolitan University.
