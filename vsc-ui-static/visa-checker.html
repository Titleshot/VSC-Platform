How scoring works

We use GPA, English scores, gap years, and finance strength. Your score (0–100) maps to a band:

  • HIGH (70+) — Strong profile, good chances
  • MODERATE (45–69) — Room for improvement
  • LOW (<45) — Focus on weak areas
